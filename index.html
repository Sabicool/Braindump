Welcome

I’m a doctor in Australia. This is the public slice of my notes: things I’ve read, argued with, or worked out for myself, kept in one linked pile rather than written up as essays. My medical notes live over here, and the pieces I’ve actually finished are on my blog.

Notes here are written for me first. Some are a paragraph I haven’t come back to; a few have been rewritten a dozen times. Each one says which it is:

Seedling
a thought I’ve had once and not tested.
Budding
worked over more than once, still moving.
Evergreen
I’ve stopped changing my mind about it

The links to notes I haven’t made public appear as plain text.

Where to start

Why this exists

Writing a note down is how I find out whether I actually think it. Publishing them is a way of making uphold a certain level of quality and output.
