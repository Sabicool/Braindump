The sacredness of medicine pertains to its intertwinement with the lives of patients

25 October 2025·Sabiqul Hoque

Medicine is seemingly treated different to other professions because it inherently deals with the livelihoods of patients and their families.

Follow On Thoughts

However, sometimes because of this attitude, those in the medical profession often suffer. In medical school for example, it seems to be the only discipline in university where there were extended periods of time on placement and where the semesters were much longer than other degrees. At other times, it seems that these expectations are re‑inforced onto doctors For example, patients’ families will want updates from the doctor at 6PM or on weekends when there may only be after hours doctors working who have no close relation with the day to day management of patients.

On the other hand it is this deep connection of the job of medicine to people’s livelihoods that makes medicine rewarding at times.
