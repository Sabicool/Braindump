Is it ethical to enjoy treating critical illness

26 January 2026·Sabiqul Hoque

Paradoxical nature of being a doctor, where life‑threatening emergencies can be thrilling for the medical team while being devastating for the patient and their family. This is different to the context in which treating illness is rewarding for the doctor and the patient. For example it’s deriving genuine enjoyment from the adrenaline rush of leading a cardiac arrest.

Follow On Thoughts

I feel perhaps at the end of the day, you would prefer the doctor who finds genuine curiosity and enjoyment from their work, rather than the one who is always sombre. In some ways, the majority of doctors who go into critical care specialtiws enjoy to some aspect the adrenaline of their job. Critisising it, is perhaps in some regard denying some doctors from derriving enjoyment from their work.
