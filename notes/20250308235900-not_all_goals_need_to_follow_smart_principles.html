Not all goals need to follow SMART principles

8 March 2025·Sabiqul Hoque

Goals do not have to follow SMART goals. Goals such as “living a peaceful life” or “having an empty calendar” are reasonable too.

Follow On Thoughts

Admittedly these goals are probably the more meaningful ones, but also the most difficult to plan and break down.

Perhaps what can help in terms of breaking down this goal can include envisioning what achieving said goal would look like and the path to achieve said goal. This can help with finding major milestones or parts of the goal. For example being a great father can be broken down into the qualities of a good father and eventually you can break it down into a daily actionable thing like: “be appreciative of one thing from your children”.

These goals are still valid but probably not the best goals to set as a quarterly goal. Mainly because there’s no distinction or defining property for its completion. Instead they probably present themselves as themes that you should remind yourself of regularly so you make random actions towards them.
