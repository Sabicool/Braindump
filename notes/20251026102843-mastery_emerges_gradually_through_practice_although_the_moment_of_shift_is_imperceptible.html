Mastery emerges gradually through practice although the moment of shift is imperceptible

26 October 2025·Sabiqul Hoque

Deliberate effort transforms into effortless skill. Most people might experience this in the context of learning a new skill such as driving a car.

Follow On Thoughts

Somewhat found this to be the case with cannulas. I was genuinely not very great at it and then slowly, I started to become better with enough practice and eventually found myself to be fairly competent at them Can take a look at my IV cannulation notes here
