Boredom

23 April 2025·Sabiqul Hoque

Not being pre‑occupied with a task, and not seeking stimulation to fill the gap. We’re quite averse to it these days.

  • Boredom can be a source of motivation
  • Removing access to distraction works better than trying to tolerate boredom
